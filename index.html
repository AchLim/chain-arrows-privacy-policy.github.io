Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 26, 2026

Chain Arrows is a minimalist puzzle game developed by NEW PARADIGM STUDIOS. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number. However, third-party services used in the game may collect certain data.

2. Advertising

The game displays ads from third-party ad networks, including:

• Google AdMob

• Unity Ads

• ironSource LevelPlay (mediation platform)

These services may collect: device advertising ID, IP address, device information, app usage data, and approximate location (based on IP).

This data is used for ad delivery, personalization, and fraud prevention.

3. In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not store or access your payment information.

4. Game Data

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. No gameplay data is transmitted to our servers.

5. Data Security

Data processed by third-party services is transmitted securely over HTTPS.

6. Children’s Privacy

This game does not target children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Third-Party Services

• Google AdMob

• Unity Ads

• ironSource LevelPlay

• Google Play Billing

Each service has its own privacy policy governing data usage.

8. Your Rights

You can limit ad personalization through your device settings. You may also reset your advertising ID at any time.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be posted on this page.
